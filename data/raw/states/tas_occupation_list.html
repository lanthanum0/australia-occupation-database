Migration Tasmania - Skilled Migration - Health, Allied Health, and Teaching Occupations

Health, Allied Health, and Teaching Occupations

The occupations below help rank registrations of interest in the Migration Tasmania Gateway.

To meet the gold priority attribute for the Tasmanian Skilled Employment, Skilled Graduate, and Established Resident pathways, candidates must have a skills assessment for an occupation listed below. They must also work in Tasmania in the same 3-digit ANZSCO group. For example, someone with a skills assessment for “241213 Primary School Teacher” could be working in any of the roles listed in the “241 School Teachers” group.

The below occupations are also eligible for Subclass 190 Overseas Applicant (Health or Education Sector Job Offer) Pathway.
